
# Universal Tech Inc. — Shipping and Returns Policy

## 1. Shipping Locations
Universal Tech Inc. ships to addresses within the United States. International shipping is not currently available.

## 2. Processing Time
Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days. Processing may take longer during holidays, major sales, or periods of high order volume.

## 3. Delivery Estimates
Standard shipping usually takes 3–7 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Weather, carrier delays, and incorrect addresses may affect delivery.

## 4. Shipping Fees
Shipping fees are displayed at checkout before the customer places an order. Any free-shipping promotion must meet the conditions stated in that promotion.

## 5. Order Tracking
When an order is dispatched, customers may receive a shipping confirmation email containing tracking information, where available.

## 6. Address Changes and Cancellations
Customers should contact support as soon as possible to request an address change or cancellation. A request cannot be guaranteed once an order has entered fulfillment or been dispatched.

## 7. Return Window
Eligible unused products may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Products must be returned in their original condition with applicable accessories and packaging.

## 8. Non-Returnable Items
Gift cards, downloadable digital products, and items explicitly marked final sale are not eligible for return unless applicable law requires otherwise.

## 9. Damaged or Incorrect Orders
Customers should contact support within 7 calendar days of delivery and provide the order number, a description of the problem, and photographs where appropriate.

## 10. Return Shipping
For a change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. If the product arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent, support will review the case and explain the available remedy.
