
# Universal Tech Inc. — Customer Support Policy

## 1. Contact Channels
Customers may contact support through the contact options published on the company's official store. This sample policy does not specify a real email address or telephone number.

## 2. Support Hours
Support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern Time, excluding public holidays.

## 3. Response Times
The target first-response time is within 2 business days. This is a target, not a guarantee. Complex cases may take longer.

## 4. Information Needed
Customers should provide their order number, the email address associated with their account, and a clear description of the issue. Customers should not provide passwords or full payment-card details.

## 5. Order Problems
Support can review reported shipping delays, missing items, incorrect products, damaged deliveries, and order-status questions.

## 6. Account Access
Customers who cannot access their account should use the official password-reset process. Support may request reasonable information to verify account ownership.

## 7. Complaints and Escalation
If an issue remains unresolved, customers may ask support to escalate the case to a supervisor. Escalation does not guarantee a particular outcome.

## 8. Privacy Requests
Customers who want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information should use the company's published privacy contact channel. Requests may require identity verification and are handled subject to applicable legal requirements.
