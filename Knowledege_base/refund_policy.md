
# Universal Tech Inc. — Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Eligibility
Refunds are available for eligible returned products after the return has been received and inspected. Approval depends on the product's condition and the return policy.

## 2. Refund Processing
After a return is approved, Universal Tech Inc. initiates the refund to the original payment method. The company generally processes approved refunds within 5–10 business days.

## 3. Bank Processing Time
The customer's bank or payment provider may require additional time to display the refund. The company cannot guarantee the exact date a bank will post the funds.

## 4. Partial Refunds
A partial refund may be considered when an eligible returned item is incomplete, damaged through customer use, or missing included accessories. Support will explain the decision.

## 5. Shipping Charges
Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable for change-of-mind returns. Different rules may apply when an order was incorrect, damaged, or subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Missing Refunds
Customers who have not received an approved refund after 10 business days should first check their payment account and then contact customer support with their order number and refund confirmation, if available.

## 7. Duplicate Charges
Customers who believe they were charged twice should contact support with the order number, transaction dates, and relevant transaction references. They should not send complete payment-card numbers or security codes.

## 8. Refund Status
Customer support can review the available order and refund status information. The policy does not guarantee instant refunds or same-day resolution.
