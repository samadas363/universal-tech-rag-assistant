
# Universal Tech Inc. — Payment and Security Information

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
Available payment methods are displayed at checkout. Availability may depend on the customer's location and the payment providers supported at the time of purchase.

## 2. Payment Processing
Payments are processed by third-party payment providers. Universal Tech Inc. may receive transaction status, payment method information, and limited transaction details needed to manage orders.

## 3. Card Information
The company does not intend to store complete payment-card numbers or card security codes in its ordinary application database. Customers should enter payment information only through the designated secure checkout flow.

## 4. Account Passwords
Account passwords should be stored using an appropriate salted password-hashing method rather than as readable plain text.

## 5. Account Protection
Customers should use unique passwords and protect their login credentials. They should contact support if they suspect unauthorized account access.

## 6. Security Incidents
Suspected security incidents are reviewed by the appropriate company personnel. Notifications and other actions depend on the incident, investigation, and applicable legal requirements.

## 7. Sensitive Information
Customers should not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, or card security codes in ordinary support messages.

## 8. Important Limitation
This document describes sample security practices for a demonstration project. It does not certify that any real company has implemented or audited these controls.
